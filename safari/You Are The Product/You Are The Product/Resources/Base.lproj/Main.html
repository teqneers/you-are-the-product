You Are The Product

See who is watching you while you browse.

Turn on the extension in Safari Settings → Extensions.

The extension is on. Open any website and click its icon in the Safari toolbar.

The extension is off. Turn it on in Safari Settings → Extensions.

What it shows

  • Who is watching: the companies a website loads, live, and which ones arrived only after you clicked “Accept”.
  • What they learn: the page you read, IDs that recognise you, products and prices, even email addresses typed into forms.
  • Cookie banner check: tracking that ran before your choice or after “Reject”, and your consent in plain words.
  • The big picture: a graph of all sites and the companies that connect them.

Try it

  1. Allow the extension on all websites when Safari asks.
  2. Open a news site, for example cnn.com or theguardian.com, and click “Accept” in the cookie banner.
  3. Click the extension icon in the toolbar and watch the counter.
It blocks nothing and sends nothing away: all data stays in Safari on this Mac.
