## 1. Privacy communities

### Hacker News ("Show HN")

Post on a weekday morning US time (about 15:00–17:00 in Germany). Title max. 80 characters. Stay around for the
first two hours to answer questions; HN values a founder who replies.

**Title:** Show HN: You Are The Product – see who tracks you while you browse, live

**URL:** https://github.com/teqneers/you-are-the-product

**First comment (by the poster):**

> I teach, and I wanted something that makes tracking visible to a class in a few minutes, the way Lightbeam once
> did. You Are The Product is a browser extension (Chrome, Firefox, Safari) that only observes: it lists the third
> parties a page loads and decodes what they receive. Some things it shows: which companies started before you
> answered the cookie banner and which kept going after "Reject", the TCF consent string in plain words, ad pixel
> events (AddToCart with price, hashed email), typed email addresses that leave a form before Submit, Prebid
> auctions ("Sold.") and session replay scripts, where you can replay your own last 30 seconds the way the vendor
> sees them.
>
> It blocks nothing on purpose; the point is to see what normally happens, then decide. Everything stays in the
> browser, there is no server. GPL-3.0, tracker data from DuckDuckGo's Tracker Blocklists.
>
> On cnn.com it counted 29 companies on one page. I'd be glad about feedback, especially on false positives.
