You Are The Product — logo concepts

Six directions for the browser extension that shows, live while you browse, which companies follow you. Every mark is drawn on a 128×128 grid with bold shapes and at most two colours: ink (light #e8eaf0 on dark, near-black #0f1115 on light) and the tracker red #ff4d5e from lib/theme.css. Each concept is shown large, as a lockup, at the toolbar sizes 16/32/48/128 px and on dark and light backgrounds. The standalone SVGs in this folder switch their ink colour with prefers-color-scheme.

Notes for picking

  • Most legible at 16 px: 1 Tagged, 4 Crosshair, 3 In the Cart. They keep one big silhouette and one accent.
  • Most "eye-opening" for a classroom: 1 Tagged and 6 Sold — the joke lands instantly, even for twelve-year-olds.
  • Most "tech / data" flavoured: 2 Network Eye and 5 Scanned — good for the website hero, weaker as a 16 px icon.
  • Concepts can be combined: e.g. the 1 Tagged mark as toolbar icon, with the 5 Scanned illustration as a hero graphic.
  • The red stays the same on light backgrounds (contrast to white about 3.2:1, enough for non-text graphics). If a stronger contrast is needed on white, #e5384a is a safe darker sibling.
